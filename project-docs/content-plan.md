## 2. Audience

- **Primary (~80% of pieces):** the *tendero* (neighborhood shop owner, segment A). Average age 43–44, 52% of shops run by women (Fenaltiendas, via La República and Portafolio); 49,55% do not use WhatsApp or social media as a commercial channel (Fenalco 2026). Tone: neighborhood, direct, "usted".
- **Secondary (occasional):** people who already enter shops: PC technicians, printer and scanner sellers, accountants (`sales-strategy.md` § 6). A good *tendero* piece also reaches them.
- **Not now:** developers ("building a POS with AI agents"). That audience does not buy the POS; it belongs to the spin-off (`sales-strategy.md` § 11) and to a separate account, so the algorithm is not confused about who to show us to.

### 3.1 Commercial name

**Proposed: Caja Clara** (Tech Lead recommendation, PO 2026-11-14: document it; **not final until the three checks below pass**). The product had no commercial name (none in `spec/product.md`).

Why it wins:

- It says the benefit in two words: the register balances and the money is clear. A 43-year-old shopkeeper gets it without an explanation.
- It covers the whole product (register, *fiado*, cashier) and the other verticals of segment C (hardware store, pharmacy, bakery); a name with "fiado" or "tienda" would lock us in.
- It is easy to say and remember (the repeated CA- sound).
- It fits faceless content: "Clara" can be the brand voice on carousels ("Clara le explica…") without the PO on camera; later the PO is "el que hizo Caja Clara".
- A web search found no POS or app in Colombia with that name (2026-11-14). That is not a trademark clearance.

Possible tagline: *"Caja Clara: la caja cuadra, el fiado se cobra."*

Rejected:

| Name | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Cuentas Claras | Most emotional ("cuentas claras, amistades largas" fits *fiado*) | A common saying: weak as a trademark, hard to get the handle; an expense app with that name already exists on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lu/app/cuentas-claras/id6447208378). **Fallback** if Caja Clara fails, with a modifier ("Cuentas Claras POS") |
| Cuadre | Shopkeeper jargon ("cuadrar caja") | In Colombia "mi cuadre" means a romantic partner or a fling |
| Mostrador | Points at the differentiator (runs on the counter PC) | Generic, says no benefit, not registrable |
| La Libreta | Nostalgia for the *fiado* notebook | Locks us into *fiado* and evokes what we replace |
| Fiao | Catchy, neighborhood tone | Locks us into *fiado*, which is no longer enough on its own (`sales-strategy.md:27`) |

Checks before it is final (PO, about 10 minutes):

1. Handles on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: `cajaclara`, or `cajaclara.co` / `cajaclarapos`; ideally the same on all three.
2. Domain: `cajaclara.co` or `cajaclara.com.co`.
3. Trademark at the SIC (Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio), free search of registered signs, Nice classes 9 (software) and 42 (online services, for the Connected plan). An identical registration in those classes means we switch names.

## 4. Content approach: educate with PAS, no face

Every piece follows **PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)**, 20–40 seconds:

1. **Problem (0–2 s), the hook:** a concrete pain with a number. *"Si usted fía, hay una plata que ya perdió y no lo sabe."*
2. **Agitate (2–20 s):** do the math on screen. *"15 clientes × $20.000 × 2 meses sin pagar = $600.000 parados. Eso es su surtido de una semana."*
3. **Solution (20–35 s):** first a free tip that works without buying anything (*"póngale fecha a cada fiado"*), then the tool (*"yo hice un sistema para eso"*), then the CTA.

**The four pains, rotated:** *fiado* that does not come back; a register that does not balance; not knowing the real profit; depending on the internet or a monthly fee.

**Formats (no face):**

| Format | PO does | Claude does |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram carousel (5–7 slides with the math) | Publish | Everything |
| Hands + voice-over (notebook, coins, drawer, scanner, POS screen) | 5-second clips and reads the voice-over | Edit, captions, hook text |
| POS screen recording (the solution) | Nothing, or one click | Record, edit, caption |

The voice is the PO's own; an AI voice sounds like an ad and lowers trust. Face on camera comes later, when the PO is ready (the founder-diary series with client #1 is held until then and until the owner agrees to be filmed).

**Weekly mix:** piece 1 educational (reach + lead magnet); piece 2 a demo of the solution in a home "test shop" (counter, scanner, printer, real products from the starter catalog of US-103) or a carousel. Instagram stories reuse the leftover clips.

The demo always shows what a phone app cannot: **works without internet, on the counter PC, with a barcode scanner, and the cashier does not see the money** (`sales-strategy.md` § 2).

## 5. Honesty rules

1. **Acted sketches yes, fake testimonials no.** A paid actor saying "this system saved me" is misleading advertising (Ley 1480 de 2011, Estatuto del Consumidor) and destroys the trust we sell.
2. **The fear must be real and countable.** The viewer can redo the math. Never "le están robando" without proof; say "así se pierde plata de la caja sin que nadie robe".
3. **No market figures that are not verified.** The figures in `sales-strategy.md` are marked unverified until R-209.
4. **No reverse-engineered or unofficial automation** of Instagram or TikTok (instagrapi and similar). It breaks the platforms' terms, and the usual penalty is losing the account, our only channel.

Wording rules:

1. Never "gratis" for the software. Say *"pago único, sin mensualidad"*, so a future free tier (R-207) is a pleasant surprise and not a change of words.
2. Never "le avisa solo". Say *"usted ve quién le debe hace más de 30 días y le cobra con un clic"*. "Solo" (automatic) is what the Connected plan sells.
3. **No prices in videos.** Prices are given on WhatsApp; a published video with an old price lives forever.
4. Never "actualizaciones para siempre". **Open contradiction:** `sales-strategy.md:61` says updates for 12 months; `docs/comercial/oferta-y-contrato.md:22` says corrections during the support period (3 months). The PO resolves it (§ 9).

What is truly given away is the *fiado* spreadsheet and the tips: they are never charged, so they carry no risk.

### 7.1 Strategies, by value

Research summary: Instagram's head (Adam Mosseri) names watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** as the main signals, with sends weighing more for non-followers (Buffer, Sprout Social). Saves and shares are the usual signal for which pieces deserve paid promotion.

| # | Strategy | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Design every piece to be forwarded**: the CTA asks to send it to a shopkeeper friend or a partner | $0 | Day 1 |
| 2 | **Cross-post to Facebook** and share in local merchant groups | $0, ~2 min a piece | Day 1 |
| 3 | **WhatsApp channel or broadcast list** "el consejo del martes" for everyone who got the spreadsheet: they do not buy the day they find us | $0 | Day 1 |
| 4 | Web calculator "¿Cuánta plata tiene en la calle?" | Hosting | Later |
| 5 | Distributors that already hold WhatsApp lists of shopkeepers (R-206) | Commission | Stage 3 |

### 7.2 Automation, in phases

| Phase | Trigger | Tooling |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Manual | Now | WhatsApp Business app: greeting message, quick replies, the lead log. Every conversation teaches what hurts |

Building phase 3 now is the most tempting option for an engineer and the one that brings the fewest customers.

### 7.3 The free *fiado* spreadsheet (lead magnet)

It is a real tool, not a crippled one: a template that is visibly broken to force a purchase is noticed and kills trust. It filters too: someone who uses Excel on a PC is our customer, because the POS runs on a PC. No phone version.

| Sheet | Content |
|---|---|
| `Movimientos` | Date · customer (drop-down) · Fiado/Abono · amount · note |
| `Clientes` | Name · phone · credit limit · balance (formula) · days since the oldest debt · color 0–14 green, 15–30 yellow, > 30 red (same buckets as `fiado.md:66`) · "Cobrar por WhatsApp" link (`wa.me` formula with the message written) |
| `Resumen` | Total money "en la calle" · how much is older than 30 days · top 5 debtors |
| `Cómo usarla` | One page, plus a 60-second video that is also a content piece |

The bridge to the product is honest: in Excel everything is written twice (sell, then note it down); in the POS the *fiado* is recorded at the sale, balances with the register, and the cashier does not see the money. The `Clientes` columns are frozen before R-219 (CSV import of customers and their *deuda anterior*, debt carried over from the paper notebook) is planned, so *"su Excel pasa al sistema en un minuto"* becomes true.

### 7.4 Lead log

A spreadsheet: date · source piece · business type · city · stage (contact → demo → sale). It tells which piece brings customers, not just views, and feeds the acquisition-cost KPI (`sales-strategy.md` § 9). Leads outside Bogotá go to a waiting list with the free spreadsheet; when remote install works (R-175, R-202) that list is the first campaign.

### 8.2 Metrics review and the switch to paid promotion

The numbers pasted every Sunday (§ 8) are read in this order. The first two decide; the rest explain.

| # | Metric | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "FIADO" messages per piece, and demos booked (lead log, § 7.4) | Does content bring conversations? |
| 2 | Sends and shares per reach | Does it travel to other shopkeepers? |
| 3 | % of viewers past 3 s | Does the hook work? |
| 4 | Saves | Was the tip useful? |
| 5 | Views and followers | Context only; never a goal (§ 1) |

There is no outside benchmark we trust for a new account in this niche, so every rule compares a piece with **our own median of the last four weeks**:

- **Winner:** "FIADO" messages or sends per reach at twice the median or more. It gets a second version (same hook, another example) and is a candidate for paid promotion.
- **Weak hook:** % past 3 s under the median. Rewrite the first sentence, keep the body, publish once more. If it fails again, drop it.
- **Week-4 review:** fewer than 3 "FIADO" messages in the 8 pieces means volume is not the problem. Stop adding pieces and change one thing: the CTA, or posting the spreadsheet directly in Facebook merchant groups. Otherwise reorder weeks 5 to 8 by pain (§ 8.1).
- **Week-8 review:** keep the two pains that brought leads, the publishing slot that won (§ 8) and the formats the PO can sustain in 60–90 minutes a week.

Paid promotion starts only when **all** of these hold:

1. Install is under 30 minutes or the remote path works (R-175, R-202); before that every extra customer is a loss (`sales-strategy.md` § 5.4).
2. At least one organic winner exists. Only winners are promoted; paying never rescues a weak piece.
3. The lead log shows at least 3 demos or 1 sale that came from content.

Then: $100.000–200.000 a month, one winner at a time, 7 days, limited to the cities where an install is possible. Stop a promoted piece that has brought no "FIADO" message after $30.000 spent (assumption, adjust with the first run), and record the spend in the lead log so the acquisition cost of `sales-strategy.md` § 9 includes it.
